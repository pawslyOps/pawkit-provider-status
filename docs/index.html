Pawkit · Operations · Proposed workflow

Provider Status

The day before a visit, the provider gets a WhatsApp reminder and the customer gets one too, unless the provider cancels. On the day, one tap on "On my way" shares the provider's location, and the customer is updated from Google Maps. If the provider goes quiet, Zoho asks the CS team to call, and the customer is kept informed either way.

In short

Day before

Reminders

Provider: reminder with Confirm / Can't make it. Customer: reminder, sent unless the provider cancels.

On the day

Live updates

Provider taps On my way. From there, Nearby and Arrived reach the customer from Google Maps with no more taps.

Provider silent

CS team steps in

CS team is alerted 45 min before to call. If the provider can't be reached, the customer gets a "checking with your provider" message.

Groomer working

Quiet times

Nothing non-urgent while a groomer is grooming or driving. Job offers and reminders wait until they tap Completed, or the 7pm batch.

One booking, one live status

A new field on the Booking record, Visit Status, holds where the visit is right now. Every change to it sends the right WhatsApp message. The existing Booking Status (Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled, To be Rescheduled) stays and is set from it at the end.

How providers are chosen

Zoho builds the offer list from the provider records. The CS team decides who is offered the job, but no longer sends the asks and chasers by hand.

Step 1 · Filter

Who can do this job

  • Right type: groomer, vet or nurse (Provider_Type)
  • Active, not Inactive or Blocked (Account_Status)
  • Handles the pet's species (Types_of_species)
  • Can handle aggressive or medical-care pets, if the notes say so (Partner_Flags)
  • Not already on a Pawkit job at that time
Step 2 · Rank

Who goes first

  • The groomer the customer asked for, or who did this pet last time
  • Nearest: from home address, or from their previous Pawkit job that day
  • Rating
  • Fair share: fewer recent jobs go ahead
Step 3 · Offer

Who gets the message

  • Customer asked for someone: only them first; if Can't or no reply, the next on the list
  • Otherwise the top 3 at once; first Can do wins
  • CS team can reorder or pick one person
Outside jobs

Providers' own jobs

Zoho only sees Pawkit jobs. An offer that clashes with their own job: they tap Can't. A slow reply while busy: the offer moves on, nobody is chased. Late because an outside job ran over: caught by GPS like traffic. Their own jobs are never tracked. Later, if clashes are common: a Busy until… button and days-off blocking.

Jobs go out one at a time from 8am to 9pm, each followed by "can u do?" and chasers, often while the groomer is grooming or driving. Groomers reply after they finish a job; groomers who drive only once they park.

Rule 1

Send when they finish a job

When a groomer taps Completed, everything waiting for them goes in one message: open job offers and tomorrow's reminder.

Rule 2

Quiet while working

Zoho knows each groomer's visits. From On my way to Completed, only emergencies get through. Everything else waits.

Rule 3

One message per job, with buttons

Price, service, pet, area and date, with Can do / Can't. The answer goes into Zoho. No "can u do?", no chasers.

Rule 4

No reply = next groomer

No reply within 2 hours (drivers: by end of day), the job goes to the next groomer, not another ping. A groomer the customer asked for gets the first offer.

Rule 5

7pm batch

Anything still waiting goes out once at 7pm in a single message. Urgent same-day or next-day jobs can skip the queue.

Drivers

Groomers who drive

A Drives yes/no on the provider record. Drivers get offers only after Completed or at 7pm, until evening to answer, and never need to touch the phone on the road: Nearby, Arrived and delays come from GPS.

How Nearby works with Google Maps

Provider's phone

Trip page

Opens from the "On my way" button. Uses the phone's GPS to send the exact location every 30 seconds while the page is open. Shows the route to the customer and an Arrived button.

Zoho

Booking record

Saves each location on the booking. Asks Google Maps for distance and drive time with live traffic every 2 minutes. Sets Nearby, Arrived or Delayed when the numbers cross the lines, and that sends the WhatsApp message.

Customer's phone

Live map link

Opens from the WhatsApp message. Google Map with the provider's pin, the house, and "arriving in 8 min". No app or login. The link stops showing the location once the visit starts.

Limit to plan for: a web page on iPhone stops sending GPS when the screen locks or the provider switches app. The trip page keeps the screen awake, and the 10-minute "no location" alert covers the gaps. The Pawkit app (phase 2) can send location in the background.

Phase 2 · optional

Pawkit app

  • Same statuses as push notifications in the iPhone app
  • Background location, so a locked phone keeps sending
  • Needs the app developer; Zoho stays the source

Why not WhatsApp live location: when a provider shares live location in WhatsApp, the business API only receives a pin, not the moving position. The exact, moving location has to come from the trip page.

Decisions needed

  • Automatic customer messages. These are fixed templates sent on a status change, like today's booking emails, with no AI writing them. Charlene to confirm they can go out without a person approving each one.
  • Customer channel. WhatsApp first (works today), app push later? Or is the app required from day one?
  • Provider buy-in. Providers must tap the buttons on every visit. Make it part of the provider agreement and count missed updates?
  • Google Maps account. Needs a Google Maps Platform key with billing on Pawkit's Google account (maps, distance with traffic, address lookup). Google gives a monthly free allowance; at Pawkit's volume (about 10 visits a day) the cost should be low, but Google's current prices need checking before sign-up.
  • Location privacy. Providers' location is shared only from "On my way" until the service starts, and is deleted after the visit. Providers agree to this in their agreement.
  • Timer values. Confirm: provider reminder after last job (7pm latest), customer reminder 18:00, CS check 08:00 visit day, on-the-way reminder at 90 min, CS call at 45 min, ETA + 10 min, job offer reply window 2 h (drivers: end of day).
  • Provider choice. First to accept wins, or does the CS team approve the pick first (e.g. a premium groomer for a fussy dog)? App bookings: keep accepting in the app, or move to the same WhatsApp offer?
  • Job offers. CS team stops sending job asks by hand and uses the offer queue. Which groomers drive? Which jobs count as urgent enough to skip the queue?
  • Existing reminder emails. Zoho already tracks provider and customer reminders sent. Do these WhatsApp reminders replace them, or run alongside?
  • Two visits close together. If a provider has two bookings in a row, replies match the next unfinished visit. OK, or should each message carry the booking reference?
