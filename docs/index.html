Pawkit · Operations · Proposed workflow

Provider Status

From the moment the CS team books a visit in Zoho to the moment it is done, the provider taps buttons in WhatsApp and the customer is told what is happening. If the provider goes quiet, Zoho asks the CS team to call and update it by hand, and the customer still gets the update.

One booking, one live status

A new field on the Booking record, Visit Status, holds where the visit is right now. Every change to it sends the right WhatsApp message. The existing Booking Status (Scheduled, Completed, Cancelled, To be Rescheduled) stays and is set from it at the end.

Awaiting provider→ Confirmed→ On the way→ Delayed→ Nearby→ Arrived→ In progress→ Completed
Can end at any point: Cancelled Needs reschedule Provider declined

WhatsApp messages to get approved

Messages that start a conversation need a Meta-approved template in WOZTELL (Utility category). Button replies from providers come back through the same Woztell logs that the urgent-alert rule already reads.

To providers (5)

  • Job alert: Confirm / Can't make it
  • Day-of reminder: On my way / Running late / Can't make it
  • Delay follow-up: 15 / 30 / 45 / 60+ min (then reason)
  • On-site: Nearby / Arrived / Service started
  • Close: Completed / Cancelled / Needs reschedule

To customers (7)

  • Provider confirmed
  • Provider on the way (+ ETA, + link in phase 2)
  • Delay with reason and new time
  • Provider nearby
  • Provider arrived
  • Visit completed
  • Visit cancelled / needs reschedule (CS team will call)

Build in phases

Phase 1 · about 1 week

WhatsApp buttons + CS fallback

  • New fields and status log in Zoho
  • 12 templates submitted to Meta
  • Zoho workflow per status change → WOZTELL send
  • Function that reads provider button replies
  • Timers and CS team alerts
  • "Live visits" board on Ops360
Phase 2

Live location + traffic ETA

  • "On my way" opens a provider page that shares phone GPS while travelling
  • Customer tracking link with map and ETA
  • Google Maps traffic ETA, refreshed every few minutes
  • Nearby fires on its own at ~2 km / 10 min
  • Delay offered to provider when ETA slips 15+ min
Phase 3 · optional

Pawkit app

  • Same statuses as push notifications in the iPhone app
  • Booking screen shows status + map
  • Needs the app developer; Zoho stays the source

Why not WhatsApp live location: when a provider shares live location in WhatsApp, the business API only receives a pin, not the moving position. Live tracking and traffic ETA need the phase 2 link.

Decisions needed

  • Automatic customer messages. These are fixed templates sent on a status change, like today's booking emails, with no AI writing them. Charlene to confirm they can go out without a person approving each one.
  • Customer channel. WhatsApp first (works today), app push later? Or is the app required from day one?
  • Provider buy-in. Providers must tap the buttons on every visit. Make it part of the provider agreement and count missed updates?
  • Timer values. Confirm 4 h to accept, reminder at 90 min, CS call at 45 min, ETA + 10 min.
  • Two visits close together. If a provider has two bookings in a row, replies match the next unfinished visit. OK, or should each message carry the booking reference?
